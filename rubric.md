# Rubric


## Impact:

The impact is for people who want to stay up to date with what is currently going on in the tech world.

## Score:

1. Minor product updates, other information that is not overly important. Example headline: “Apple may factor parking into Apple Maps arrival time estimates”
2. interesting information, company information which would have limited reach. Example headline: “Sony retires the PlayStation Plus PC app, which goes offline December 1
3. Important information about the tech world that will affect a lot of developers and tech users such as an emerging technology that has a lot of potential. Example headline: “Microsoft November 2026 Patch Tuesday fixes a record 1,235 flaws, including an actively exploited Outlook zero-day”
4. Tech News that is not overly important but is something that someone who is interested in the tech world would want to know, Maybe a new device from a major company or major update. Example headline: “Starlink announces the Mini 2 satellite internet terminal on its website”
0”
5. Major Tech News that will affect the world, This is usually news that people who are not overly interested in the tech world will hear about, such as new revolutionary technology or a major cyberattack, leak or law. Example headline:“Hackers stole $6.3 billion from Binance in the largest crypto heist ever, with about $3.13 billion still in linked wallets”
